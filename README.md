## Full Stack Software Engineer | ZK & Privacy Engineer

We live in exciting times, blockchain technologies allow us to build applications that put users first. The world computer needs to be permissionless, auditable, censorship-resistant, open-source and have native privacy in order for the humanity to trust it and build critical applications on top of it. My goal is to push the world computer dream forward.

### Experience ​👩‍🚀🚀

- **Software Engineer at Privacy Stewards of Ethereum (PSE)**
    - [State of Private Transfers dashboard](https://github.com/privacy-ethereum/private-transfers-benchmarks): evaluations and benchmarks of +55 protocols accross 35 properties from privacy, decentralization, censorship-resistance and compliance.
    - [zkWormholes](https://github.com/privacy-ethereum/superchainerc20-zkWormholes): a private transfer approach that allows pausible deniability (nobody will know you used a private transfer protocol)
    - [Post quantum UTXO-based private transfers](https://github.com/NicoSerranoP/pq-shielded-pool): Notes (UTXOs) are the best approach (until today) to do private transfers on-chain because of its adaptability for other smart contracts (e.g. DeFi)
    - [Post quantum HE private transfers](https://github.com/NicoSerranoP/pq-private-transfer): HE (Homomorphic Encryption) allow for efficient state storage (every private interaction only modifies the encrypted balance. There are no nullifiers or notes created. Displaying the balance is easier than note-based approaches)
    - [MACI](https://github.com/privacy-scaling-explorations/maci): Minimal Anti Collusion Infrastructure proposed by Vitalik Buterin. It is a private voting open-soruce protocol used by several organizations (e.g. Gitcoin)
    - [KZG Ceremony](https://github.com/zkparty/trusted-setup-frontend): the frontend to contribute to the KZG ceremony required to scale Ethereum through BLOBs (Danksharding)
    - [p0tion](https://github.com/privacy-scaling-explorations/p0tion): a trusted setups toolkit so developers can easily run their trusted setup ceremonies in their infrastructure
    - [DefinitelySetup](https://github.com/privacy-scaling-explorations/DefinitelySetup): the frontend of p0tion used in [ceremony.pse.dev](https://ceremony.pse.dev/)
