## 1. Tokenized U.S. Treasuries & Money-Market Funds

The largest and most institutional RWA category (~$7B+ on-chain AUM in early 2026, up from ~$850M two years prior).

### BlackRock — BUIDL
- **Description:** BlackRock USD Institutional Digital Liquidity Fund, launched March 2024 on Ethereum with Securitize as transfer agent. The largest tokenized treasury product (~$2.4–3.0B in 2026); entered DeFi rails in early 2026. The reference product of the category.
- **Source:** Eco explainer; Intellectia market report
- **URL:** https://eco.com/support/en/articles/15483226-what-is-buidl-blackrock-s-tokenized-treasury-fund · https://securitize.io

### Franklin Templeton — BENJI / OnChain U.S. Government Money Fund
- **Description:** The first U.S.-registered mutual fund to use a public blockchain for its official share register; runs on Ethereum, Stellar, and others with daily NAV on-chain. In 2026 also launched tokenized ETFs accessible 24/7 via Ondo Global Markets.
- **Source:** Eco tokenized treasury comparison; 4irelabs RWA guide
- **URL:** https://www.franklintempleton.com/investments/options/money-market-funds · https://eco.com/support/en/articles/15210582-top-tokenized-treasury-funds-2026-buidl-ousg-usdy-benji-compared

### Ondo Finance — OUSG & USDY
- **Description:** Short-Term US Treasuries Fund (OUSG, institutional) and US Dollar Yield token (USDY, non-US retail). OUSG's portfolio migrated in 2024 to a stack of BUIDL + USYC + Superstate USTB for instant liquidity. One of the top RWA platforms by TVL.
- **Source:** Eco tokenized treasuries comparison
- **URL:** https://ondo.finance · https://eco.com/support/en/articles/15002232-tokenized-treasuries-compared

### Superstate — USTB
- **Description:** Short Duration US Government Securities Fund from Robert Leshner's (Compound founder) Superstate; also a launch collateral asset on Aave Horizon.
- **Source:** Eco comparison; Aave Horizon launch coverage
- **URL:** https://superstate.co

### Hashnote — USYC
- **Description:** US Yield Coin, a tokenized short-duration treasury/repo product; acquired by Circle (2025) and used as a liquidity leg inside Ondo OUSG and derivatives-exchange collateral.
- **Source:** Eco tokenized treasuries comparison
- **URL:** https://usyc.hashnote.com

### WisdomTree — WTGXX / WisdomTree Prime
- **Description:** Asset manager WisdomTree's tokenized Government Money Market Digital Fund and a broader retail platform of tokenized funds on Ethereum and other chains.
- **Source:** Stablecoin Insider top-10 tokenized treasury funds
- **URL:** https://www.wisdomtree.com · https://stablecoininsider.org/top-10-tokenized-treasury-funds-in-2026-buidl-benji-and-the-highest-yielding-on-chain-options/

### Spiko
- **Description:** European tokenized money-market funds (EU T-bills), among the top RWA platforms by TVL in 2026 — the leading euro-denominated entry in a dollar-dominated category.
- **Source:** Chainstack top RWA platforms 2026
- **URL:** https://www.spiko.io · https://chainstack.com/top-rwa-tokenization-platforms-2026/

### BNP Paribas Asset Management — AssetFoundry
- **Description:** Issued a tokenized money-market fund share class directly on public Ethereum via the bank's in-house AssetFoundry™ tokenization platform — a marker of European bank adoption of public chains.
- **Source:** InvesTax Q1 2026 RWA market report
- **URL:** https://investax.io/blog/q1-2026-real-world-asset-tokenization-market-report

## 2. Tokenization Platforms & Compliance Infrastructure

### Securitize
- **Description:** SEC-registered transfer agent + broker-dealer; the issuance platform behind BlackRock BUIDL and Apollo's tokenized funds. $4B+ tokenized across 580k investor accounts; supports Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum, Avalanche, Plume, and more.
- **Source:** Eco RWA platform comparison; CoinGape platform review
- **URL:** https://securitize.io · https://eco.com/support/en/articles/15254028-best-tokenized-rwa-platforms-2026-securitize-ondo-backed-centrifuge-compared

### Tokeny (ERC-3643 / T-REX)
- **Description:** Luxembourg-based issuance and lifecycle-management platform; creator of the T-REX protocol standardized as ERC-3643 — the dominant permissioned-token standard for regulated securities on Ethereum. Acquired by Apex Group.
- **Source:** Chainstack platform review; ERC3643 Association
- **URL:** https://tokeny.com · https://www.erc3643.org

### ERC-3643 Association
- **Description:** Industry body maintaining the ERC-3643 standard; $32B+ in RWAs tokenized with the standard across 180+ jurisdictions. Members include DTCC, Apex Group, Invesco, and Franklin Templeton; cited by name by SEC Chairman Paul Atkins (July 2025). Pursuing ISO standardization.
- **Source:** ERC3643 Association news; Ledger Insights
- **URL:** https://www.erc3643.org · https://www.ledgerinsights.com/dtcc-joins-rwa-tokenization-body-erc3643/

### DTCC — ComposerX & tokenization pilot
- **Description:** The largest U.S. securities clearinghouse. Joined the ERC-3643 Association (March 2025), integrating the standard into its ComposerX tokenization platform; runs a three-year tokenization pilot under an SEC no-action letter (Dec 2025) allowing DTC-custodied assets on approved blockchains, rollout from H2 2026.
- **Source:** crypto.news; InvesTax Q1 2026 report
- **URL:** https://www.dtcc.com · https://crypto.news/dtcc-joins-erc-3643-association-to-advance-ethereum-based-tokenized-securities/

### tZERO, Zoniqx, Polymath, STOKR
- **Description:** Additional regulated issuance/ATS platforms: tZERO (SEC-regulated alternative trading system), Zoniqx (tokenization infrastructure), Polymath (ERC-1400 multi-asset), STOKR (Luxembourg, top-6 by TVL in 2026).
- **Source:** CoinGape and Chainstack platform reviews
- **URL:** https://www.tzero.com · https://www.zoniqx.com · https://polymath.network · https://stokr.io

## 3. Private Credit & Structured Credit

### Centrifuge
- **Description:** Flagship structured-credit tokenization protocol — invoices, mortgages, treasuries, consumer loans ($300M+ tokenized). Long-standing collateral partner of MakerDAO/Sky; tokenized Janus Henderson products are launch collateral on Aave Horizon.
- **Source:** BingX blockchain ranking; Eco platform comparison
- **URL:** https://centrifuge.io

### Maple Finance
- **Description:** Institutional credit marketplace tokenizing corporate/institutional debt; zero losses since 2023 with loans >150% collateralized; also runs Maple Cash (treasury product) and syrupUSDC. On Ethereum, Base, Solana, Arbitrum.
- **Source:** Eco platform comparison; Chainstack review
- **URL:** https://maple.finance

### Goldfinch
- **Description:** Early undercollateralized private-credit protocol on Ethereum, now focused on Goldfinch Prime — bringing funds from managers like Apollo and Ares on-chain for qualified investors.
- **Source:** Clapp Finance RWA platforms overview
- **URL:** https://goldfinch.finance · https://clapp.finance/blog/rwa-gold-rush-10-platforms-tokenizing-real-world-assets-in-2026

### Apollo — ACRED (via Securitize)
- **Description:** Apollo Diversified Credit Securitize Fund: a tokenized feeder into Apollo's diversified credit strategy — a marquee example of a major alternative-asset manager issuing on-chain.
- **Source:** FluidRWA top tokenization companies
- **URL:** https://www.fluidrwa.com/blog/top-tokenization-companies-2026 · https://securitize.io

## 6. Tokenized Bonds & Fixed Income

### European Investment Bank (EIB)
- **Description:** Issued the first-ever digital bond on public Ethereum (€100M, April 2021, "Project Mars" with Goldman Sachs, Santander, SocGen); settlement in ~60 seconds vs. T+2. The template for public-chain sovereign-grade issuance.
- **Source:** EIB press release
- **URL:** https://www.eib.org/en/press/all/2021-141-european-investment-bank-eib-issues-its-first-ever-digital-bond-on-a-public-blockchain

### UBS, Siemens, Société Générale — digital bond issuance
- **Description:** UBS's CHF 375M tokenized bond (2022); Siemens' €60M Polygon bond (2023) and €300M SWIAT bond (2024) under Germany's eWpG electronic-securities law. Combined tokenized bond issuance crossed $10B in early 2026. UBS also runs UBS Tokenize (money-market fund uMINT on Ethereum).
- **Source:** Coinpaprika tokenized bonds explainer; Global Wisdom analysis
- **URL:** https://coinpaprika.com/education/tokenized-bonds-how-world-bank-and-eib-issue-bonds-on-blockchain/ · https://www.ubs.com

## 8. DeFi Integration of RWAs

### Sky (formerly MakerDAO)
- **Description:** Largest crypto-collateralized stablecoin issuer (DAI/USDS, ~$5.4B TVL on Ethereum). RWA holdings crossed $1.5B in early 2026 and are the protocol's largest revenue source, allocated via Centrifuge, BlockTower, and other partners.
- **Source:** Eco Sky architecture explainer
- **URL:** https://sky.money · https://eco.com/support/en/articles/14796323-inside-sky-dai-and-usds-architecture

### Aave Horizon
- **Description:** Aave Labs' institutional RWA market (launched Aug 2025): institutions borrow USDC, RLUSD, and GHO against tokenized collateral (Superstate funds, Circle's yield fund, Centrifuge/Janus Henderson products). The clearest bridge between regulated funds and DeFi liquidity.
- **Source:** Aave launch blog; CoinDesk coverage
- **URL:** https://aave.com/blog/horizon-launch · https://www.coindesk.com/business/2025/08/25/aave-labs-debuts-horizon-to-let-institutions-borrow-stablecoins-against-tokenized-assets

### Ethena, Plume, and RWA-focused chains
- **Description:** Plume is an EVM RWA-focused chain used by Securitize and others; several Ethereum-aligned L2s (Base, Arbitrum) host the bulk of new RWA issuance outside mainnet.
- **Source:** Chainstack platform review
- **URL:** https://plume.org · https://chainstack.com/top-rwa-tokenization-platforms-2026/

## 10. Institutional Privacy Layer (EthSystems' arena)

### EthSystems
- **Description:** For-profit spinout of the Ethereum Foundation's Institutional Privacy Task Force (launched July 14, 2026). Founders: Mo Jalil (CEO, ex-Goldman Sachs), Oskar Thorén (Waku/Logos privacy infrastructure), Aaryamann Challani. Anchor funding from Bitmine Immersion (NYSE: BMNR), SharpLink (Nasdaq: SBET), and Joe Lubin. Thesis: confidentiality, not scalability, is the barrier keeping banks off public Ethereum; approach: zero-knowledge proofs that enforce AML/sanctions compliance cryptographically without revealing amounts or counterparties. Sister EF spinouts: EthLabs, Ethereum Institutional.
- **Source:** CoinDesk launch and thesis coverage; Decrypt; Bankless
- **URL:** https://www.coindesk.com/tech/2026/07/14/ethereum-foundation-spinout-ethsystems-targets-banks-with-blockchain-privacy-technology · https://www.coindesk.com/tech/2026/07/28/ethereum-startup-ethsystems-bets-privacy-is-key-to-getting-banks-on-public-blockchains · https://decrypt.co/373523/team-behind-ethereums-institutional-privacy-push-spins-out-for-profit-firm-ethsystems

## Market snapshot (mid-2026)

- Total RWA on public chains: **~$30–31B**, up 400%+ since early 2025; Ethereum holds **~51–65%** of tokenized value depending on methodology.
- Tokenized T-bill funds: **$7B+** AUM. Tokenized commodities: **~$7.4B** (gold-dominated). Tokenized equities: **~$2.4B** (July 2026). Tokenized bonds: **$10B+** cumulative issuance.
- Direction of travel: DTCC pilot rollout (H2 2026), Morgan Stanley tokenized equities (late 2026), banks moving from private chains to public Ethereum/L2s — with confidentiality as the last major blocker, which is exactly the gap EthSystems targets.
